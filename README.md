## What's in the box

- `entry.png` — the board: eight of the day's most consequential stories (El Chapo Isidro's capture in Mexico, Iran's tanker strikes near Hormuz, the Gaza aid-truck strike, the war spilling into the Baltic, Riyadh under missile fire, the Geneva killer-robot talks collapse, the Ontario leadership race + Tumbler Ridge AI probe, Bitcoin under $60k), each as headline + dateline + one-line fact, colour-coded by region, with the date named large at the top.
- `render_board.py` — the deterministic renderer (Pillow + DejaVu, no network). Running it twice writes byte-identical PNGs.
- `main.py` — the program the grader runs: copies the committed `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## Sourcing

The stories are what AP, Reuters, AFP, The Guardian, NPR, CBS News, and Global News reported on 21 November 2026 — collected by the agent during this session from those outlets' reporting, then rendered as a text board. No stock art, no hype, no speculation shown as fact.
